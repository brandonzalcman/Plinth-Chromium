The first public build of Plinth Chromium: Plinth's calm way of browsing, on Chromium, with Chrome extensions.

- **Pages in a bottom dock or a sidebar.** Switch between them in Settings. Sites fade when you leave them and go to the shelf on their own.
- **Chrome extensions.** Extensions run as they do in Chrome, and their buttons sit in Plinth's toolbar.
- **Pins, folders, groups and split view.** Drag a page onto another to split, and drag the divider to resize.
- **On-device AI.** Ask about the page (⌘J), search history by meaning, and get a "where you left off" card when a site comes back from the shelf.
- **Bring your browsing with you.** Import bookmarks, history and passwords from Safari, Chrome, Firefox and other browsers.
- **Privacy.** Always use HTTPS, block third-party cookies and send Global Privacy Control.
- **Points & Levels, peek (⇧-click a link), a mini window for links from other apps, and a welcome guide.**
- **Updates itself.** Plinth Chromium checks for new versions, or choose Plinth > Check for Updates.
