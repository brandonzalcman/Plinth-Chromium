Videos on X now play.

- **Videos on X and other sites that use H.264.** Plinth Chromium's engine can't decode H.264 or AAC, the formats X and many other sites use, so those videos said "the media could not be played". Plinth now plays them with macOS's own video player, right where the video sits in the page. They autoplay muted as they do on X, pause when you scroll away, and have macOS's playback controls, including picture in picture and full screen.
- **"Not now" on a group suggestion works first time.** One click quiets suggestions for those sites for a day, instead of showing the next combination of the same sites.
